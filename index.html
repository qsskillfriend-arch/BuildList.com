BuildList.com is operated by Sharplink Ventures (U) Limited, a company registered in Uganda (URSB registration number [insert], TIN [insert]), with its registered office at [insert address], Kampala. In this policy "we" and "us" mean Sharplink Ventures (U) Limited.

This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and what you can require us to do about it. It is written to comply with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021.

1. Two kinds of people this policy covers

We handle data about two different groups, and the rules differ for each.

Listed businesses

If your business appears in our directory, we publish information about it: the business name, category, district and area, a telephone number, and where supplied an email address, website, description, logo and photographs. Where the person named is a sole trader or where a personal mobile number is used as the business number, that information is personal data and this policy applies to it.

Visitors and subscribers

If you browse the site, submit an enquiry, request a quotation, or subscribe to our digest, we handle the data you give us and limited technical data described in section 4.

2. What we collect, and why

  • Business listing data — name, category, location, contact details, description, images. Purpose: to publish a directory that helps customers find suppliers. Lawful basis: consent, which we obtain before publishing.
  • Enquiry and quotation data — your name, telephone number, email and the details of what you need. Purpose: to pass your request to the suppliers you selected. Lawful basis: performance of the service you asked for.
  • Digest subscription data — email address and, if given, name and firm. Purpose: to send the weekly digest. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Listing submission data — everything on the sign-up form, including the name and role of the person submitting. Purpose: to verify and publish the listing. Lawful basis: consent.
  • Technical data — see section 4.

3. Where listing data comes from

We are explicit about this because it matters. Listings reach us three ways:

  1. Submitted by the business through our sign-up form, with consent recorded at the point of submission.
  2. Collected by our field team in person, with consent recorded on the visit.
  3. Compiled from publicly available sources — public professional registers, published directories and businesses’ own public advertising. Where a listing was compiled this way, we contact the business by telephone to confirm the details and obtain consent before publishing it. A listing that has not cleared that call is not published.

4. Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are used and which listings receive enquiries. This records technical data including approximate location derived from IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and actions such as tapping a telephone number. IP anonymisation is enabled.

We record, per listing, how many times its page was viewed and how many times a visitor tapped its contact button. We share these figures with the listed business, because they are about that business. We do not share the identity of the visitor, because we do not hold it.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not operate behavioural retargeting, and we do not sell data to advertisers. Advertisers on this site receive counts of impressions and clicks, never visitor identities.

You can block analytics in your browser settings or with any standard blocker. The site works normally with analytics blocked.

5. Who we share data with

  • Suppliers you choose — when you submit a quotation request, it goes to the suppliers shown to you before you sent it, so they can contact you directly.
  • Service providers — our website host (Netlify), form processor, analytics provider (Google) and email platform. Each processes data on our instructions.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not supply our listing database or subscriber list to third parties.

Some of these providers are located outside Uganda. Where data is transferred abroad we rely on the provider’s contractual data protection commitments, as permitted under section 19 of the Act.

6. How long we keep it

  • Published listings: for as long as the listing is live, plus 12 months after removal for audit purposes.
  • Consent records: for the life of the listing plus 6 years, because they are our evidence that publication was lawful.
  • Quotation requests: 24 months.
  • Digest subscriptions: until you unsubscribe, then we keep only your email on a suppression list so we do not accidentally add you again.
  • Analytics: 14 months, Google’s default retention.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you may ask us to:

  • Remove your listing. We action removal requests within 48 hours. You do not have to give a reason.
  • Correct anything inaccurate. Usually done the same day.
  • Tell you what we hold about you, and provide a copy.
  • Stop sending you the digest — every message carries an unsubscribe link.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, which for a listing means removal.

Contact hello@buildlist.com or WhatsApp +256 700 000 000. If you are not satisfied with our response you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Office, National Information Technology Authority – Uganda (NITA-U), Kampala.

8. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Administrative access is restricted to named staff. We do not store payment card details at any point; where payment is taken it is handled by a licensed payment provider. We keep the minimum data necessary, which is itself a security measure.

If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the Personal Data Protection Office as required by the Act.

9. Children

This is a business-to-business service and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes

If we change this policy materially we will update the date above and, for changes affecting listed businesses or subscribers, tell you directly.
